Teri

You want them to describe the broken journey themselves. Don't describe it for them.
Teri
"Roughly how many enquiries do you get a week? And of those, how many actually book?"
If they don't know the number, that itself is the signal — no system, no visibility, no control.
Teri
"What happens when you're not available to respond to a DM straight away?"
Gets them to name the leak. They feel this already — you're just giving it language.
What to listen for in Round 2
  • Frustration about missed messages or slow responses — that's your anchor
  • Uncertainty about conversion numbers — means they have no system at all
  • Any mention of clients or renters "who went elsewhere" — emotional hook
